What gets published

Once cross-site results land in Azure Blob, the FLAIR aggregator stacks per-site reports into a single paper deliverable:

  • TRIPOD-AI table (per task) with one row per site — discrimination + calibration + decision-curve.
  • FairCareAI table (per task) with per-site × per-subgroup AUROC and parity gaps.
  • Cross-site curves — overlaid ROC / PR / calibration, one line per site.
  • Cohort summary — yearly counts from extract-metadata, showing the leaderboard's denominators.

That bundle is what becomes the NEJM AI paper + supplement.
