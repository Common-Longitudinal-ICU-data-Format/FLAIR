Clinical motivation

A continuous severity signal lets you order patients by expected oxygenation burden — useful for trial enrollment, RT staffing, and prone-positioning candidate identification. Cleaner than a binary "ARDS yes/no" label, which is famously hard to define.

Why it's hard

  • Label denominator varies — short-stay patients have fewer SpO₂ readings, noisier rates.
  • SpO₂ devices and sampling intervals differ per site (continuous monitor vs. q1h spot check).
  • Distribution is heavy at zero; mean-squared error punishes the tail disproportionately.
