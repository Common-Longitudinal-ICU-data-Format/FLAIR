Reintubation within 72h (decision-point)

At every extubation event that closes a ≥ 24h IMV episode, predict whether the patient will need a new IMV episode within 72 hours.

Note on the input window. The window above is the minimum guaranteed lookback, not a cap. The model is free to use any data observed before the prediction time — including data from previous hospitalizations of the same patient. Only data at or after the prediction time would constitute leakage.

Clinical motivation

Failed extubation roughly doubles ICU mortality. A reliable 72h reintubation risk score lets the team weigh the extubation timing — and identifies patients who'd benefit from prophylactic NIV / high-flow nasal cannula instead of a "wait and see" extubation.

Why it's hard

  • Cohort is small — extubation episodes are a slice of all ICU stays.
  • Decision-point framing depends on accurate episode detection — IMV start/stop consensus rules are intricate (see _ventilation_episodes.py).
  • Trach/end-of-record exclusions trim the cohort sharply; sites with high trach rates have small denominators.
