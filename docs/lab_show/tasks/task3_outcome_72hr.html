Clinical motivation

The 24-72h window is when most ventilator weaning decisions get made. A reliable three-way signal helps RT and intensivists set expectations, plan SBT timing, and trigger goals-of-care conversations for the trajectory toward death.

Why it's hard

  • Multiclass with severely imbalanced rare class (expired).
  • Liberation timing depends on local weaning protocol — site variation will drive label noise.
  • The 24h prerequisite ("must be on IMV at hour 24") is what makes this task tractable but also reduces sample size sharply.
