Clinical motivation

LTACH placement is operationally heavy — bed availability is scarce, family conversations are different, payer auth takes days. A reliable 24h signal lets the discharge planner start early instead of on day 14.

Why it's hard

  • Extreme class imbalance — LTACH disposition is rare (often < 5%).
  • Site variation is huge: some hospitals have on-campus LTACH partners, others ship out.
  • HIPAA suppression hits harder here — small subgroups → many "<10" cells in fairness.
