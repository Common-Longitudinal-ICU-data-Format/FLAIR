Clinical motivation

Mortality risk-stratification at 24h is the workhorse outcome — used for triage, goals-of-care timing, ICU performance benchmarking (e.g., SMR), and as the de facto comparator for any new severity score (SOFA, APACHE, …).

Why it's hard

  • Calibration matters as much as discrimination — uncalibrated mortality probs lead to bad triage.
  • Site case-mix drives prevalence: MIMIC's 13.8% is at the high end vs. community-hospital sites.
  • Hospice transfers blur the label edge — sites disagree on which "comfort care" pathways count.
