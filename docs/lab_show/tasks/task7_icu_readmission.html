Unplanned ICU Readmission

At ICU discharge (transfer out to floor), predict whether the patient bounces back to any ICU within the same hospitalization.

Note on the input window. The window above is the minimum guaranteed lookback, not a cap. The model is free to use any data observed before the prediction time — including data from previous hospitalizations of the same patient. Only data at or after the prediction time would constitute leakage.

Clinical motivation

ICU bounce-back is a CMS-tracked quality metric and a direct sign of premature transfer. A reliable bounce-back probability at the transfer-out order lets the team negotiate downgrading vs. holding the bed — and audit transfers that go wrong.

Why it's hard

  • Variable-length input window (full ICU stay) — unlike T1–T6's clean 24h window.
  • "Unplanned" definition varies: some sites exclude planned post-procedure ICU returns, some don't.
  • Survivor bias is structural — patients who die before transfer are not in the cohort.
