Intubation within 6h (decision-point)

At every hour the patient is in the ICU and not yet intubated, predict whether they will be on confirmed IMV within the next 6 hours. One row per hour until first IMV.

Clinical motivation

Intubation is one of the most consequential ICU decisions — delaying it harms; doing it preemptively also harms. A reliable 6h-ahead probability gives the bedside team a "watch closely" prompt while keeping the human in the loop. Decision-point framing means the score is fresh, not a one-shot at admission.

Why it's hard

  • Class imbalance per row is severe — most hourly rows are negative.
  • Two metrics matter: per-row rate AND per-patient ever-positive rate (different operating points).
  • Label-leakage risk: respiratory_support rows around the intubation moment can leak the future if the window isn't strict.
  • IMV consensus rules (≥ 15 min) drive label noise — site-by-site documentation patterns matter.
