Clinical motivation

Bed turnover is the rate-limit on ICU throughput. A 24h LOS estimate at every admission lets capacity-planning roll up to a unit-level forecast — and at the patient level, it sets discharge-planning urgency.

Why it's hard

  • Long right tail — a handful of patients stay weeks; their absolute error dominates RMSE.
  • Operational signal at first 24h is sparse for short-stay patients.
  • Survivorship: censored patients (transferred out, died) have no clean LOS — handled via cohort filter, but limits the cohort.
