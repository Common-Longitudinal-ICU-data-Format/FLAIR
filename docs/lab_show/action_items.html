Open questions

  • Do we publish per-site results in the paper or aggregate-only? Sites' preference matters.
  • Should the count-vector baseline include time-of-day / day-of-week features, or stay strictly count?
  • What is the threshold for "beat the baseline" — Δ AUROC > 0.02 with non-overlapping bootstrap CI?
  • Does the FLAIR compliance test need a separate "site-readiness" check (e.g., parquet exists, timezone set)?
